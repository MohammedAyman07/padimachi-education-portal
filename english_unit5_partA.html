Part A: Extensive Language for Work Place

1. Choose the right Collocation and fill the blanks:

  1. I always avoid his company because he is a crashing bore.
  2. It is a golden opportunity. If you miss it, you will regret it.

2. Choose the right Collocation and fill the blanks:

  1. Although I was slightly annoyed by her attitude, I said nothing.
  2. Could you check the oil?

3. Choose the correct verb that agrees with the subject:

  1. All of the CDs, even the scratched one, are in this case.
  2. Three Idiots is fiction.

4. Choose the correct verb that agrees with the subject:

  1. Either answer is acceptable.
  2. The man with all the birds lives on my street.

5. Choose the correct verb that agrees with the subject:

  1. Eight dollars is the price of a movie these days.
  2. Good friendship keeps your mind and body healthy.

6. Expand the following compound noun:

  1. Generator Room: A room for a generator
  2. Energy source: A source of energy

7. Expand the following compound noun:

  1. Electricity generator: A generator that produces electricity
  2. Steam turbine: A turbine that runs on steam

8. Match the Informal Vocabulary with Formal Substitutes:

  • Leave out - Omit
  • Go against - Appose
  • Deal with - Handle
  • Stand for - Represent

9. Choose the formal usage of word for appropriate meaning:

  1. Every individual has rights which should be respected.
  2. Consuming organic food is good for our health.

10. Match the Informal Vocabulary with Formal Substitutes:

  • Beverage - a drink
  • Sufficient - enough
  • Comprehension - understanding
  • Subsequently - later
