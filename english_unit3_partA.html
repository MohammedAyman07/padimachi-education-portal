Part A: Introduction to Formal Writing

1. Fill in the blanks with the suitable form of the verb:

  1. I received a medal for the race yesterday.
  2. The girl was playing piano yesterday.

2. Change the given sentences to Passive voice:

  1. The flowers are being watered by Vivek every day.
  2. The tomato soup was prepared by my mother.

3. Change the given sentences to Active voice:

  1. The barrel can can the can.
  2. The mentor was advising the student.

4. Change the given sentences to Impersonal passive voice:

  1. The automatic traffic monitoring device was installed by the government.
  2. My instrument box has been stolen.

5. Fill in the blanks with appropriate Preposition:

  1. The dog is grateful to its owner.
  2. My brother’s wedding anniversary is on 5th November.

6. Choose the appropriate word from the given options:

  1. My grandfather was a heavy smoker, so few people were surprised when he died of oral cancer.
  2. She was a devoted wife who loved her husband more than anything else in the whole universe.

7. Fill in the blanks with appropriate Preposition:

  1. The opponents sat opposite to each other.
  2. The scientist looked through the microscope.

8. Fill in the blanks with the suitable form of the verb:

  1. A factory with modern equipment is for sale.
  2. We will paint our bedroom next month.

9. Change the sentences to passive voice:

  1. The house was destroyed by the storm.
  2. My ideas will be presented at the conference tonight.

10. Find out the word which has cloze meaning with the underlined word:

  1. It is possible, with current technological capabilities, to transmit...
    d. send
  2. Wireless computer networking is also similar to cellular phone use...
    d. alike
