Part A: Enhancing Speaking Ability

1. Frame Wh-questions for the following sentences:

  1. The children are playing in the garden.
    Ans: Where are the children playing?
  2. Peter runs with his dog on Sundays.
    Ans: When does Peter run with his dog?

2. Frame Wh-questions for the following sentences:

  1. He studies Economics at the university. (what)
    Ans: What does he study at the university?
  2. My parents have two cars. (how many)
    Ans: How many cars do your parents have?

3. Frame Yes/No-questions for the following sentences:

  1. They went to the swimming pool.
    Ans: Did they go to the swimming pool?
  2. She wastes her money on jewelery.
    Ans: Does she waste her money on jewelery?

4. Frame Yes/No-questions for the following sentences:

  1. He decided to leave his wife.
    Ans: Did he decide to leave his wife?
  2. They should revise their lessons.
    Ans: Should they revise their lessons?

5. Add proper Question Tag:

  1. Your uncle hadn't met him before, had he?
  2. The bus stop is over there, isn't it?

6. Add proper Question Tag:

  1. None of those customers were happy, were they?
  2. Jenni eats cheese, doesn't she?

7. Replace the underlined word with its Synonym:

  1. His character was of the highest caliber.
    Ans: c. quality
  2. A chameleon camouflages itself by changing color.
    Ans: a. Disguise

8. Replace the underlined word with its Antonym :

  1. Rakesh is an eccentric PRODIGAL.
    Ans: d. thrifty
  2. Friendship is always RECIPROCAL.
    Ans: b. one-sided

9. Frame sentences using the given words as noun and verb:

1. Object

  • (Noun): The police found a strange object at the crime scene.
  • (Verb): I object to your last statement.

2. Produce

  • (Noun): The farmer sells fresh produce at the market.
  • (Verb): The factory will produce 10,000 units this year.

10. Frame sentences using the given words as noun and verb:

1. Watch

  • (Noun): My father gave me a new watch for my birthday.
  • (Verb): We will watch a movie tonight.

2. Bark

  • (Noun): The bark of the tree was rough.
  • (Verb): The dog will bark at the mailman.
