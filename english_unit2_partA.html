Part A: Listening and Extended Reading

1. Rewrite the sentences with the tense given in the bracket:

1. The boy speaks the truth. (Present Continuous Tense)

The boy is speaking the truth.

2. I have finished my assignment. (Past Perfect Tense)

I had finished my assignment.

2. Write the Sentence Structure of the following sentences:

1. The team is playing very well.

Subject (The team) + Verb (is playing) + Adverbial Phrase (very well) (Structure: S + V + Adv)

2. The school will be closed for winter break.

Subject (The school) + Verb (will be closed) + Adverbial Phrase (for winter break) (Structure: S + V + Adv)

3. Insert the suitable Articles in the blanks:

______ oranges I brought were very sour.

The oranges I brought were very sour.

Sharja will take______ train from ______ next stop.

Sharja will take the train from the next stop.

4. Choose the right One word Substitutes for the following sentences:

1. A person interested in collecting, studying and selling of old things -

(a) Junk dealer (b) Archeologist (c) Crank (d) Antiquarian

(d) Antiquarian

2. Policeman riding on motorcycles as guards for VIPs -

(a) Outriders (b) Commandos (c) Servants (d) Attendants

(a) Outriders

5. Fill the blanks with correct Phrasal Verbs:

1. Please _______ the lights when you leave.

Please turn off the lights when you leave.

2. The flight will _______ any moment now.

The flight will take off any moment now.

6. Fill the blanks with Cause and Effect Expressions:

1. She made one big mistake ______ she lost her job.

She made one big mistake and as a result she lost her job. (Other answers like 'so' might also fit depending on context)

2. ______ the electricity went out for most of the day, the ice cream in the freezer melted.

Because / Since the electricity went out for most of the day, the ice cream in the freezer melted.

7. Fill in the blanks with the suitable form of the verb:

1. That bottle _______ (belong) to me.

That bottle belongs to me.

2. Freddy will be _______ (go) to work tomorrow.

Freddy will be going to work tomorrow.

8. Fill in the blanks with the suitable form of the verb:

1. I _____ (go) to school everyday.

I go to school everyday.

2. They will _______ (come) to the party tomorrow.

They will come to the party tomorrow.

9. Fill in the blanks with the suitable Article:

1. My son is _______ apple of my eye.

My son is the apple of my eye.

2. _______ history professor who taught us is retiring today.

The history professor who taught us is retiring today.

10. Fill the blanks with correct Phrasal Verbs:

My car _______ in the middle of the road. (broke down/broken down)

My car broke down in the middle of the road.

The kid decided to _______ a new hobby. (take up/take on)

The kid decided to take up a new hobby.
